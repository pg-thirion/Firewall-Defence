    Field manual

    Survive 15 waves in campaign mode or defend indefinitely in endless mode. Threats travel along the highlighted route. Deploy defences on adjacent tiles. Block threats to earn credits and upgrade nodes to level 3.

    Defence in depth

    Firewalls intercept ordinary traffic. IDS detects and marks stealth malware but does not block it by itself. Rate limiters slow threats. IPS blocks detected threats, WAF counters web exploits, and scrubbers damage clusters. Combine them for maximum coverage.

    Repairs cost 60 credits and restore up to 20 integrity between waves. Selling returns 65% of total investment. The simplified mechanics are educational abstractions, not real firewall behaviour.
